THE LAST PAGE OF OUR LITTLE STORY

A Little Note ♡

Dear memories,

I returned home, but a little part of those days stayed with me.

The production line, the coffee conversations, the Instagram messages, the long calls, the gifts, and that drizzling evening at the railway station.

Some moments were ordinary when they happened. Only later did I realise how special they had become.

It was never a story that needed a name. It was simply a bond, and I wanted to preserve it here.

With a smile,
The girl who remembers ♡

Not every memory needs a tomorrow.
Some are beautiful just as they were.
♡
